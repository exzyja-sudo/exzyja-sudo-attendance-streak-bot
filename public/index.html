01 / The rhythm

One reaction.
A visible promise.

01

Check in daily

At your chosen time, the bot posts a fresh attendance message. React with ✅ to keep your place.

02

Build your run

Every check-in extends a personal streak. Shields can cover a single missed day without breaking the momentum.

03

Let the roles speak

When someone misses too long, the bot moves them to inactive and can announce the change in a channel you choose.

04

Restore streaks and reset shields

Moderators can use /restore-streak and /forgive-inactive to bring a member back, reset their shield state, and refresh the live attendance view instantly.

05

Boxed announcements and birthday greetings

Use /announcement for read-confirmation posts, and /birthday for a boxed celebration message in the configured announcement channel.

06

Schedule the moments ahead

Plan one-time, daily, weekly, or yearly announcements, including automatic birthday celebrations in the channel and timezone you choose.

07

Ask the room

Open a poll channel to a selected role, collect 🟢 and 🔴 reactions, then announce the winner before closing access again.

03 / The toolkit

Small commands.
Clear signals.

Everything your moderators and members need stays close to the conversation.

COMMANDWHAT IT DOES
/setup-attendanceChoose channel, time, roles, timezone, and the announcement channel
/announcementPost a boxed read-confirmation announcement with a required ✅ reaction
/meetmeCall a member to the Meeting room for 20 minutes, then announce assignment and expiry
/schedule-announcementSchedule one-time, daily, weekly, or yearly announcements and birthday celebrations
/pollOpen access for a role, collect 🟢 and 🔴 votes, then announce the outcome and hide the channel
/streaksShow the community leaderboard with live streak and shield details
/my-streakCheck your current streak and shields privately
/restore-streakRestore a member's streak, wipe shield state, and post a boxed streak-restored notice
/forgive-inactiveRemove the inactive role, restore saved roles, and re-show the active attendance state
/birthdayPost a boxed birthday greeting to the configured announcement channel
Manual role updateAnnounce a temporary ON HOLD status when inactive is assigned by hand

05 / Ready when you are

Midnight in Manila.
Momentum everywhere.

Use the values below to start a daily attendance ritual for your server.

SETUP / ASIA-MANILA
channel: #attendance
announcement-channel: #attendance-alerts
time: 00:00
timezone: Asia/Manila
enable-role-automation: true
inactive-role: @Inactive
exemption-roles: @Staff, @Admin

The bot saves each member's last active access roles when they check in and restores them after forgiveness. Need a different reset? Keep the timezone and change time to any 24-hour value.
